Privacy policy

Contact Manager accesses Google Contacts only for its owner's own Google accounts, and only after the owner grants access on Google's consent screen.

  • Contact data is stored only on the owner's computer and is sent only to Google.
  • No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
